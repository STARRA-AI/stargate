# Clone a Profitable App With AI and Flip It (TrustMRR + Blink)

**Source:** Aria Codez

**Date Added:** July 27, 2026

## Overview

The play is simple: find a small app that is already making money, build your own better version with AI in minutes, and flip it. No idea needed, no code needed.

**Three steps:**
1. Find it on TrustMRR
2. Build it on Blink
3. Flip it on Acquire.com or Flippa

---

## Step 1: Find a Money-Making App (TrustMRR)

Go to [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com). It lists real startups and apps with their actual monthly revenue.

**How to pick:**
- Sort by revenue **high to low**
- Look for a **SIMPLE** app with one core feature
- Already making recurring revenue (higher MRR = more proven demand)
- Weak or dated design you can easily beat
- Broad audience (not a tiny niche)

**What makes an app worth cloning:**
- One simple core feature you can describe in a sentence
- Already making recurring revenue
- Weak or dated design
- Broad audience

Copy the app’s description — you will paste it into the build prompt next.

---

## Step 2: Clone It With Blink

Go to [Blink](https://blink.new/?via=ariacodez) (use the link for $10 free credits).

**Exact prompt to paste:**

```
Build a production-ready full-stack web app. Here is exactly what it does:

[PASTE THE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION you copied from TrustMRR here]

Build all of this:
- A clean, modern landing page with the core tool front and center, better designed than a typical competitor.
- The main feature working end to end.
- Email and password auth, plus a backend database that saves each user and their results.
- A pricing page with a free tier and two paid tiers, with Stripe checkout fully wired up.
- Programmatic SEO pages: auto-generate "compare" and "[my app] vs [competitor]" pages and "best [category] tools" pages so it ranks on Google.
- Fully mobile responsive, fast, and deployable as is.

Make it cleaner and more polished than the original. Deliver it working in a single shot.
```

---

## Step 3: What the Prompt Builds

Within minutes you get a fully functional app that usually looks cleaner than the original. It includes:

- Working product with **auth and a real backend database**
- **Pricing tiers with Stripe checkout** already wired up
- Programmatic **SEO compare pages** for free Google traffic

This combination turns a clone into something sellable, not just a demo.

---

## Step 4: Flip It

List the finished app on:
- [Acquire.com](https://acquire.com)
- [Flippa](https://flippa.com)

Buyers pay for ready-made, working apps because it saves them months of building. The more it shows (clean product, some traffic, Stripe ready), the more it is worth.

---

## Bonus: Make It More Sellable

Before listing, do these:

- Get a handful of real users (even free) — traction sells
- Publish the compare/SEO pages so a buyer sees Google traffic potential
- Connect Stripe and leave it ready (buyers love turnkey checkout)
- Write a one-page summary: what it does, the idea, and the room to grow

---

## FAQ

**Is this legal?**  
You are building your own version of a proven idea with your own code, brand, and design. Do not copy their exact name, logo, or copyrighted content.

**Do I need to code?**  
No. Blink writes and runs all the code from the prompt.

**Is it free to start?**  
Blink has a free tier + $10 credits via the link. TrustMRR browsing is free. Listing on Acquire/Flippa is free.

**How much do these sell for?**  
It varies with revenue and traffic. Even apps with no revenue yet have value because they save the buyer the build time.

**What makes a good app to clone?**  
One simple core feature, already making recurring money, broad audience, and weak design you can beat.
