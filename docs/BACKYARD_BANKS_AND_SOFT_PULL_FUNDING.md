## Part 1: Backyard Banks – 17 Hidden Institutions

### Why Backyard Banks Matter
- Relationship-based underwriting (humans still review files)
- Many do **not** report ongoing business activity to personal credit
- Lower competition = cleaner approvals
- Soft-pull or single-bureau edges available

### Key Terms
- **Soft Pull (SP):** Does not affect score
- **Hard Pull (HP):** Formal inquiry (5–10 point drop possible)
- **Personal-bureau reporting:** Whether balances show on personal credit (non-reporting cards are gold for utilization strategy)
